Controls card draw speed. In 1 Player mode, also affects computer aggressiveness.

Things you should know:

  • Tap the screen before you begin to change the color of your sticky hand.
  • During the game, tap any of the rule icons to pause the game and read a list of rules in play.
  • Jokers don't do anything unless the rule to slap jokers is in play.
  • Aces are considered consecutive to 2 and K.
